Keepr for Google Messages

This extension lets the Keepr desktop app copy your texts from Google Messages for Web. It does nothing on its own:

  • It works only when you click Sync Android in Keepr, and only on messages.google.com.
  • When you sync, it reads ALL your conversations from the period set in Keepr and sends them to the Keepr app on this computer only.
  • You agree to this in Keepr itself, before your first sync. Keepr explains there what it keeps, how texts are linked to transactions, and how to delete them.
